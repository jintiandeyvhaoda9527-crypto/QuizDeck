# Third-party notices

QuizDeck is distributed under the MIT License, but its dependencies remain subject to their own licenses and notices.

Principal runtime components include React, Next.js/vinext, Capacitor, and SheetJS Community Edition. Build and test tooling includes Vite, TypeScript, ESLint, Tailwind CSS, and their transitive dependencies. The lockfile is the authoritative dependency-version record for this source tree.

Before distributing a binary, maintainers should generate a production dependency license report from the tagged commit, review packages with copyleft or attribution requirements, and include any notices required by the exact resolved versions. License metadata in package registries is informative; the license files shipped by each dependency remain authoritative.

No third-party question-bank content is required by QuizDeck. Repository fixtures and the demo bank must be original, synthetic, public-domain, or supplied under a compatible license with attribution.
